Executive Business Recommendations & Strategic Monetization

Pillar 1: Content Investment & Retention

Observation: Movies comprise 69.7% of the catalog, but TV series generate higher watch-time and lower monthly subscriber churn.

Action: Allocate 40% of commissioning budget into serialized multi-season series in target territories.

Pillar 2: Regional Market Expansion

Observation: India catalog has 1,057 titles with 92.3% movie bias, leaving TV episodic consumption under-penetrated.

Action: Launch regional episodic originals tailored to South Asian and Latin American markets.

Pillar 3: Demographic Tiered Pricing

Observation: Adult content accounts for 45.6% of titles, while family/kids content represents 21.6%.

Action: Introduce discounted 'Family & Kids' ad-supported subscription tiers to capture multi-viewer households.

Pillar 4: Staggered Release Scheduling

Observation: 28.4% of catalog additions are dropped on Fridays, cannibalizing viewer engagement across titles.

Action: Spread mid-tier documentaries and indie titles to Wednesday drops to sustain continuous daily active users.
